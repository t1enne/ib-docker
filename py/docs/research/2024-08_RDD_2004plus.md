# RDD HMM+CPI leading-indicator re-run on the full SPY 2004+ history

**Status:** Reimplementation of the documented `rdd_lead.py` flip-level + series-level
lead study, run on the newly-extended SPY daily sample (2004-01 → 2026-08, 5667 bars —
**double the macro cycles** of the original 2014+ window). Checked in as
`scripts/rdd_lead_2004.py`.

**Repro:** `uv run python scripts/rdd_lead_2004.py [--fit-mode expanding|full]`

## Why reimplement

The runnable artifacts the docs describe (`scripts/rdd_lead.py`, `rdd_lead_*.py`,
`src/bt/strategies/rdd_regime.py`) were **never committed** — `git log --all` for those
paths is empty. Only the pure feature module `src/bt/strategies/rdd.py`,
`MarketRegimeHMM` (`src/indicators/hmm/hmm.py`), and the surviving series-level harness
`scripts/lead_scan.py` exist. This script re-derives the documented method and applies
the same statistical discipline (`block_bootstrap_p`, tradeable band, EARLY/LATE) that
downgraded the claim at 2014+.

## Method (documented, faithfully reproduced)

1. **Features** (`src.bt.strategies.rdd`): `z_acc` (2nd-diff log CPI, smoothed w_a=63),
   `z_real` (real equity level), `z_rdisc` (10y yield − trailing CPI). Defaults
   `w_a=63, k=252, w_z=252`. Both variants `{acc,real}` and `{acc,real,rdisc}`.
2. **Regime model** (`MarketRegimeHMM` → 2-state GaussianHMM, diag cov, random_state=42):
   two fit modes.
   - `expanding` (default, cursor-safe): refit every 50 bars, training data up to cursor
     only (no lookahead) — the honest version.
   - `full`: single fit on the whole standardized matrix (matches docs' "reference run").
3. **Forward-return relabel** (the documented deviation from `rank_states_by_vol`): order
   the 2 states by mean forward return at h_anchor=10 so **regime0 = higher fwd return
   (risk-on)**, regime1 = risk-off. State identity tracked across refits by overlap
   matching; a `relabel_conflicts` counter records refits where the new fit's fwd-return
   ordering contradicts the tracked labels.
4. **Persistent flips**: transition between two runs each held ≥ min_run=5 bars (flicker
   screen). 62 flips (CPI-only) / 37 (with-rdisc) on the full sample — a healthy n vs the
   original 10–26.
5. **Lead-time study (§4.1)**: for each flip, trading-day lead to the next index turning
   point (flip→risk-off: next local max; flip→risk-on: next local min), pivot detection
   ±5 bars with a 3% min-swing screen; median + Q1/Q3 + frac(≤20d) + frac(coincident<3d).
6. **Discrimination (§4.2)**: mean forward return per regime, gap = r0 − r1, per h.
7. **Series-level lead-lag (README Follow-up Test B)**: corr(regime0_t, fwd_{t→t+h}),
   corr(regime0_t, past_{t→t−h}), block-bootstrap premium and p. This is the test that
   killed the claim at 2014+.

## Verdict on the original 2014+ conclusion

**The extended sample changes the CPI-only picture, but does not clear the significance
bar — and carries a serious methodological confound.**

| | 2014+ (original) | 2004+ (this run, expanding) |
|---|---|---|
| `{acc,real}` corr(regime0, r_+5..+15) | **−0.02..−0.03** (negative) | **+0.081** (positive) |
| `{acc,real}` discrimination @21 | small / unstable (EARLY −0.23% → LATE +0.54%) | **+0.98%**, EARLY +1.66% / LATE +0.25% (same sign) |
| `{acc,real,rdisc}` forward edge | zero significant | zero significant (runs negative) |
| flip sample | 10–26 (small-n, low confidence) | **62** (adequate) |

Two correct readings:
1. **Direction flipped.** On the doubled sample, the CPI-only regime's forward correlation
   went from *negative* (2014+) to *positive* (+0.08 band, +0.11 @21, +0.13 @42), the
   discrimination gap turned positive and monotone, and the EARLY/LATE split no longer
   flips sign. This direction of change is *not* what a pure overfit artifact predicts —
   more independent cycles moved it away from zero, not toward a remembered peak.
2. **Still not statistically significant.** Every block-bootstrap p ≈ 0.50. Under the
   project's own autocorrelation-honest bar, the positive corr is within the noise floor
   (the same 0.45+ floor `combo_lead_scan.py` found). **You cannot yet claim a real lead
   on this sample either — it is now "suggestive but not significant" rather than "null".**

**The confound that cuts against both readings: `relabel_conflicts` 63/103 (CPI-only).**
In 61% of refits the forward-return ordering of the two states *contradicted* the tracked
labels. The documented "regime relabel instability" is severe on the shorter refit rhythm.
If the regime0/1 semantic flips around inside the run, both the discrimination gap and the
series-level corr are measured on partially-mislabeled points, and the +0.08 band number
could be inflated or inverted by that mislabeling. **Before trusting the sign flip, the
relabel must be made stable** — e.g. anchor regime0 by a stable emission-parameter (CPI
acceleration level) instead of per-refit forward return, which the docs themselves flagged
as the fragile choice.

## Honest bottom line

- **Claim:** "RDD HMM+CPI regime *consistently leads* SPY turning points."
- **Verdict: REFINE (not DEAD).** The 2014+ "concurrent filter, no lead" conclusion does
  **not** survive the extended sample in the same form — the CPI-only signal flipped to a
  positive, split-stable forward correlation with an adequate flip sample. But it is
  **still not significant** under the block bootstrap, and the state-relabel instability
  (61% of refits) is a confound that must be eliminated before the +0.08@band number is
  believable.
- **Next single change (one A/B, not a sweep):** make the state anchor **stable** — relabel
  regime0 by a *fitted emission parameter* (e.g. lower mean CPI-acceleration = cost-push
  easing = risk-on), not per-refit forward return — and re-run the identical harness. If the
  +0.08 band corr and +0.98% @21 gap survive with ≤5% relabel conflicts, the lead has a
  stable footing and is worth taking to a tradeable-horizon overlay test. If the sign
  flips again, the +0.08 was a label-identity artifact.

## Addendum (2026): stable-anchor A/B — the sign-flip was a label-identity artifact

Repro: `scripts/rdd_lead_2004_anchorab.py` (12 unit tests in
`scripts/tests/test_rdd_lead_2004_anchorab.py`).

Because the expanding run had **61% relabel conflicts** (63/103 refits) under the
per-refit forward-return anchor, the +0.081 band corr / +0.98% gap@21 could have been
a label-identity artifact. A single-variable A/B swapped ONLY the state-relabel anchor
(it must be a fitted emission parameter, not a per-refit sample statistic):

| Anchor | corr(regime0, r_+5..+15) | gap@21 | flips | conflicts/n_refits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: mean z_acc emission** (cost-push → risk-on) | **+0.021** | **+0.28%** | 45 | **53%** |
| B: return-variance (stock HMM control) | +0.001 | −0.05% | 30 | 37% |
| fwd_return header (fresh each refit) | −0.001 | +0.03% | 32 | 39% |
| `{acc,real,rdisc}` under anchor A | +0.003 | +0.14% | 48 | 27% |
| *original harness (carried-label fwd-return)* | ***+0.081*** | ***+0.98%*** | *62* | *61%* |

**VERDICT: label-artifact (—), RDD CPI-only stays REFINE/DEAD.** The +0.081 band corr
and +0.98% gap@21 do **not** survive any stable-parameter anchor. Under the cleanest
anchors they collapse to ≈0 (+0.021 / +0.28%) at block-bootstrap p ≈ 0.53–0.60 (noise),
and no anchor achieves the ≤5% stability bar (53/37/39/27% conflicts). Two structural
facts:
1. **No anchor is stable** — the label instability is structural to the expanding-refit
   2-state HMM on daily macro z-scores, exactly as the original §11.4 concluded ("not a
   parameter choice").
2. **The sign-flip was a carried-label artifact** — it depended on propagating the first
   fit's labels forward; relabeling fresh from fitted parameters at every refit shrinks
   the edge to noise under every anchor.

**Conclusion for the research line:** the doubled 2004+ sample does **not** rescue RDD
CPI-only as a leading indicator. Close this family. The informative next tests remain
the different-signal-family proposals (Alternative A intraday-shape, Alternative C
flip-hazard) from the research review — not more macro-feature/HTF variations.

## Comparison caveat vs original run

The original docs' numbers (27–42d median lead, n=15 flips) came from the *single-fit /
`--fit-mode full`* regime at 2014+, while this report emphasises the cursor-safe
`expanding` mode. They are not directly comparable: different fit discipline. Both modes
are reported above so the comparison is explicit, not silently mixed.
